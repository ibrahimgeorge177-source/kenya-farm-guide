Finding Information on the Website

You can explore the website for practical information before contacting us. The main sections cover maize farming, other crops, farm costs, agricultural technology and farm calculators.

The calculators provide simple estimates for yield, costs, revenue, profit and cost per acre. They are intended for planning and educational purposes and should be considered alongside actual farm records and current local conditions.
