About the Guide

About Kenya Farm Guide

Kenya Farm Guide is an independent agriculture information platform created by Ibrahim Nyangino.

The goal is to bring together practical farming information, planning resources and simple digital tools that can help people better understand different aspects of farming.

The platform focuses on accessible information rather than replacing professional agricultural advice or local knowledge. Farming outcomes can vary significantly with location, weather, soil, crop variety, management practices and market conditions.
