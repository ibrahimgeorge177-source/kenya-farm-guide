PRACTICAL FARMING

Simple Practices That Support Better Farm Decisions

📝 Keep Farm Records

Record seed, fertilizer, labour, crop protection, harvesting and other production expenses.

🌧️ Monitor Weather

Pay attention to rainfall and other weather conditions when planning planting and crop management activities.

🌱 Understand Your Soil

Soil condition and fertility can influence crop performance and should be considered when planning nutrient management.

📊 Compare Seasons

Comparing yields, costs and farm practices across seasons can help identify changes and areas for improvement.
