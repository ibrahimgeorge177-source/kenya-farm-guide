Welcome

A Practical Resource for Farmers

Farming decisions involve more than planting a crop and waiting for harvest. Farmers need to think about land, seed, fertilizer, labour, weather, pests, expected yields, market prices and production costs.

Kenya Farm Guide is designed to bring some of this information together in a simple format that is easy to explore and useful when planning a farming season.

Agriculture and Technology

How Technology Can Support Farming

Agriculture is increasingly connected to digital information. Farmers and agricultural organizations can use data, mobile tools, weather information and digital records to support farm management.

Technology does not replace practical farming knowledge. Instead, useful digital tools can help organize information and support better planning.

About Kenya Farm Guide

Built to Make Farming Information Easier to Explore

Kenya Farm Guide is an independent agriculture information platform created by Ibrahim Nyangino. The platform brings together practical farming information, planning resources and simple digital tools in one place.

The goal is to make useful agricultural information easier to find and understand while encouraging farmers to consider their own local conditions, records and available agricultural advice.
