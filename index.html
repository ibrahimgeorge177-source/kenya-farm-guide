FARM PLANNING TOOLKIT

Plan Your Farm Step by Step

Good farm planning starts with understanding what you want to grow, what it may cost and what the expected returns could look like. Use the Kenya Farm Guide resources in a simple step-by-step process.

1. Choose Your Crop

Start by learning about the crop you intend to grow, including planting practices, production factors and common management considerations.

View crop guides →

2. Plan Your Farm

Consider the cultivated area, required inputs, labour and other expenses before starting the production cycle.

Plan farm costs →

3. Calculate Costs

Estimate production costs and calculate how much your farming activity may cost per acre.

Calculate production costs →

4. Estimate Returns

Use expected production and selling price to estimate potential revenue and the amount remaining after costs.

Estimate revenue & profit →

5. Check Break-Even

Find the approximate selling price required to cover your production costs for a planned quantity.

Calculate break-even price →

6. Keep Learning

Use farming articles and technology resources to improve your understanding and review your plans as conditions change.
