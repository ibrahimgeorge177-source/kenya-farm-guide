FARM PLANNING TOOLS

Simple Farm Calculators

These simple tools can help farmers estimate crop yield, production costs, revenue, profit and cost per acre.

Enter your own farm information to produce an estimate. The results are intended for planning and educational purposes and should be interpreted using current local prices and actual farm records.

HOW IT WORKS

Using the Farm Calculators

1. Enter Your Information

Use actual figures from your farm records whenever possible.

2. Check the Result

The calculator will perform the basic calculation and display an estimated result.

3. Compare Your Records

Use the result together with your farm records, production conditions and current market information.

CALCULATION METHODS

What the Calculators Measure

Yield per Acre

Harvested grain ÷ Area harvested = Yield per acre.

Total Farm Cost

Seed + fertilizer + labour + other production expenses = estimated total cost.

Revenue

Quantity sold × Selling price per kilogram = Estimated revenue.

Profit

Estimated revenue − Total production cost = Estimated profit.

Cost per Acre

Total farm cost ÷ Farm area = Estimated cost per acre.

Important Note About Estimates

These calculators perform simple mathematical calculations. They do not predict future prices, crop yields or farm profitability.

Actual farm results can be affected by weather, soil conditions, input prices, labour costs, pests, diseases, market prices and many other factors.

Use current local information and your own farm records when making financial or production decisions.
