Farm Calculators for Kenyan Farmers

Use these simple tools to estimate crop yield, production costs, expected revenue, profit and cost per acre.

Practical Farm Planning Tools

Enter your own farm figures to get quick estimates. These calculators are designed to support farm planning and record keeping.

Crop Yield Calculator

Estimate your crop yield per acre from the total harvested grain and cultivated area.

Farm Production Cost Calculator

Add your major production expenses to estimate the total cost of your farming operation.

Revenue and Profit Calculator

Estimate expected revenue and the amount remaining after production costs.

Cost Per Acre Calculator

Estimate how much you are spending for each acre based on your total farm costs and cultivated area.

How to Use the Calculators

  1. Enter the figures from your farm records.
  2. Check that the units are correct.
  3. Click the relevant calculation button.
  4. Use the result as an estimate for farm planning.

Important: These tools provide estimates based on the information entered. Actual farm results can vary because of weather, input prices, crop performance, market prices and other farming conditions.
