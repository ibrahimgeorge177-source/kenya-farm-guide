Farm Calculators for Kenyan Farmers

Simple tools for estimating yield, production costs, revenue, profit and other farm planning figures.

Practical Farm Planning Tools

Enter your own farm figures to generate quick estimates. The tools are designed to support planning, budgeting, record keeping and farm decision-making.

🌽 Crop Yield Calculator

Estimate how much crop you harvested per acre.

💰 Farm Production Cost Calculator

Add your major farm expenses to estimate total production cost.

📈 Revenue & Profit Calculator

Estimate expected revenue and the amount remaining after production costs.

🌱 Cost Per Acre Calculator

Estimate your average production cost for each acre.

⚖️ Break-Even Price Calculator

Estimate the minimum selling price per kilogram needed to recover your production costs.

🌾 Seed Requirement Calculator

Estimate the amount of seed required for a given cultivated area using a chosen seed rate.

🧪 Fertilizer Requirement Calculator

Estimate fertilizer quantity from your cultivated area and chosen application rate.

How to Use These Calculators

  1. Enter figures from your farm records or planning estimates.
  2. Check that the units are correct.
  3. Click the relevant calculation button.
  4. Review the result and use it as part of your farm planning.

Important: These calculators provide estimates based on the figures entered. Actual results can vary depending on weather, soil conditions, crop performance, input prices, market prices and other farming conditions.
