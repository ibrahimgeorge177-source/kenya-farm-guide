How to Use These Tools

A simple process can help you get more useful estimates.

1. Gather Your Figures

Check your farm records, receipts, expected harvest, cultivated acreage and planned input rates.

2. Enter Your Numbers

Enter the figures into the calculator that matches the decision you are trying to make.

3. Use the Estimate

Use the result when planning your farm budget, production requirements or expected returns.

Important Planning Note

These calculators provide simple mathematical estimates. Actual farm results can change because of weather, soil conditions, crop variety, pests, disease, input prices, labour costs, market prices and other local conditions. Always consider current local information before making major farm decisions.
