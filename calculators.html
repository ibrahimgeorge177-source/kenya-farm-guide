Farm Calculators for Kenyan Farmers

Simple tools to help you estimate crop yield, farm costs, expected revenue, profit and cost per acre.

Why Use Farm Calculators?

Farming decisions often involve numbers. Knowing how much you spend, how much you harvest and what you could earn can help you understand the performance of a farming enterprise.

These calculators provide quick estimates using the figures you enter. They are intended for planning and record keeping, not as guarantees of actual farm performance or market prices.

Understanding the Calculations

Maize Yield

Estimated yield per acre is calculated by dividing the total harvested grain by the area harvested.

For example, if a farmer harvests 2,000 kg from 2 acres, the estimated yield is 1,000 kg per acre.

Farm Cost

Total farm cost is the sum of the expenses entered into the calculator, such as seed, fertilizer, labour and other costs.

Farm Profit

Estimated revenue is calculated from expected production multiplied by the expected selling price.

Estimated profit is then calculated as expected revenue minus total farm costs.

Cost Per Acre

Cost per acre is calculated by dividing the total farm cost by the total number of acres being considered.

Important:

These tools provide estimates based on the information you enter. Actual farm results can change because of weather, input prices, labour costs, crop performance, pests, diseases, post-harvest losses and market prices.

Keep Your Own Farm Records

Calculators become more useful when you have accurate farm records. Keep receipts, labour expenses, input quantities, planting dates, harvested quantities and selling prices.

Over several seasons, these records can help you compare different crops, identify major expenses and understand how your farm is performing.
