Understanding Maize Farming

Maize is an important food and cash crop for many farmers in Kenya. However, the results from a maize farm can vary considerably depending on weather, soil conditions, planting decisions, seed selection, crop management and input use.

The guides below focus on practical questions that farmers and agricultural learners commonly ask. They can also help farmers organize their records and understand the main factors that influence production and profitability.

Practical Maize Farming Reminders

Keep Farm Records

Record seed, fertilizer, labour, land preparation, harvesting costs and final production. Good records make it easier to understand farm performance.

Consider Your Local Conditions

Maize production varies between areas. Consider local rainfall, soils, elevation, planting seasons and recommended varieties when making decisions.

Measure Results

Do not rely only on the number of bags harvested. Record the actual quantity harvested and the area cultivated so that yield can be compared accurately.
