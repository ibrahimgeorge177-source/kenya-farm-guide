MAIZE FARMING IN KENYA

Practical Maize Farming Information

Maize is one of the most widely grown food crops in Kenya. Successful production depends on several factors including seed selection, planting time, soil fertility, rainfall, spacing, weed management, pests and diseases.

This guide brings together practical information to help farmers understand important maize production activities, farm costs, yield and profitability.

FARM MANAGEMENT

Important Maize Production Considerations

🌱 Seed Selection

Choose seed varieties that are appropriate for the farming environment, growing conditions and intended use.

🌧️ Rainfall and Weather

Rainfall timing and distribution can affect crop establishment, growth, flowering and grain filling.

🧪 Soil Fertility

Soil fertility influences crop development and should be considered when planning nutrient management.

🐛 Pests and Diseases

Regular field monitoring can help farmers identify pest and disease problems early and respond appropriately.

🌿 Weed Management

Managing weeds during important crop growth stages can reduce competition for water, nutrients and light.

📊 Farm Records

Recording production costs and harvested quantities makes it easier to evaluate farm performance across seasons.

Use Local Agricultural Recommendations

Farming conditions vary across Kenya. Soil type, altitude, rainfall, seed variety and local production systems can affect the appropriate farming practices.

Use this website as general agricultural information and consider guidance from qualified agricultural extension officers and locally appropriate recommendations when making farm decisions.
