ABOUT THE PLATFORM

About Kenya Farm Guide

Kenya Farm Guide is an independent agriculture information platform created by Ibrahim Nyangino to provide practical, accessible and useful farming information for people interested in agriculture in Kenya.

The platform brings together farming guides, crop information, farm planning resources, cost information and simple digital tools that can help farmers and agriculture learners better understand common farming decisions.

WHAT WE PROVIDE

Practical Agriculture Information

🌽 Crop Information

Learn about maize and other crops through practical guides covering planting, production, management, costs and other important farming considerations.

💰 Farm Planning

Understand common farm expenses, production costs, revenue and basic profit calculations to support better farm planning.

📊 Digital Tools

Use simple calculators and digital resources to estimate yield, costs, profit and other useful farm measurements.

💻 Agricultural Technology

Explore how data, digital tools, artificial intelligence and modern technology can contribute to agricultural planning and decision-making.

OUR APPROACH

Making Farming Information Easier to Understand

Agricultural information can sometimes be difficult to find, compare or understand. Kenya Farm Guide aims to present farming topics in a clear and practical format so that visitors can quickly understand the main ideas and explore topics in more detail.

The platform focuses on practical information rather than presenting itself as a replacement for agricultural professionals, extension officers, agronomists or other qualified specialists.

Farming conditions vary between regions and farms. Soil, rainfall, altitude, input prices, labour costs, pest pressure, crop varieties and market conditions can all affect results. For that reason, information on this website should be considered alongside local conditions and appropriate professional advice where necessary.
