ABOUT KENYA FARM GUIDE

Practical Agriculture. Better Farm Decisions.

Kenya Farm Guide is an independent agriculture information platform created by Ibrahim Nyangino to make practical farming knowledge easier to understand, use and apply.

About Ibrahim Nyangino

I am Ibrahim Nyangino, the creator of Kenya Farm Guide. I developed this platform around a simple idea: useful agricultural information should be practical, understandable and accessible.

Kenya Farm Guide brings together information on crop production, farm costs, agricultural technology, farm planning and simple calculation tools. The aim is to help farmers, students, agriculture enthusiasts and other readers better understand the decisions involved in farming.

My interest in agriculture and technology led me to explore how data and digital tools can be used to make agricultural information more useful. Kenya Farm Guide is part of that effort — combining practical farming topics with simple digital resources that people can use in their own planning.

The Purpose of Kenya Farm Guide

Farming involves many decisions, from selecting crops and planning inputs to managing labour, estimating production costs and understanding potential returns.

Kenya Farm Guide is designed to bring these topics together in one place and explain them in a straightforward way. Instead of presenting agriculture as complicated theory, the platform focuses on practical concepts, examples, calculations and useful planning information.

The long-term goal is to build a reliable and useful agriculture resource that continues to grow with more farming topics, tools and data-driven resources.
