FARM PLANNING

Choosing Crops for Your Farm

Crop selection involves more than looking at potential yields. Farmers can consider several factors before committing land and resources to a crop.

🌦️ Climate

Consider rainfall patterns, temperature, growing seasons and other local environmental conditions.

🌱 Soil

Soil type, fertility, drainage and other soil characteristics can affect crop performance.

💧 Water

Water availability is important when planning crops, especially where rainfall may be unreliable or irrigation is required.

💰 Market

Consider expected demand, selling opportunities, transport and market access before planting.

👨‍🌾 Labour

Different crops require different amounts of labour during land preparation, planting, management and harvesting.

📊 Production Costs

Estimate seed, fertilizer, labour, crop protection, harvesting and other costs before starting production.

FARM RECORD KEEPING

Keep Records When Comparing Crops

Keeping records of planting dates, inputs, labour, production costs, harvested quantities and selling prices can help farmers compare different crops and seasons.

Good records can also make it easier to calculate production costs, revenue and estimated profit.

Consider Local Farming Conditions

Farming conditions differ across Kenya. Information that works in one location may need to be adapted to another because of differences in rainfall, altitude, soil, markets and farming systems.

Farmers should consider locally appropriate agricultural recommendations and seek advice from qualified agricultural extension professionals where necessary.
